# Roadmap

Lumi's direction is an AI website concierge for service businesses: answer visitor questions from business knowledge and help them take a useful next step.

This is a proposed direction, not a schedule. Discuss priorities in [GitHub issues](https://github.com/nieve-consulting/lumi/issues), and see the [releases](https://github.com/nieve-consulting/lumi/releases) for what has shipped.

## Delivered: repeatable adoption

Another business can run the same product without maintaining a company-specific fork:

- Business identity, agent guidance and branding are configured in an onboarding wizard and the admin interface, without source changes; see [Configuration](https://docs.lumi.nieve.ai/configuration).
- Administrators sign in with email and password, or optionally Google Workspace; see [Admin access](https://docs.lumi.nieve.ai/configuration/admin-access).
- A container installation installs and upgrades the complete product; see [Self-hosting](https://docs.lumi.nieve.ai/self-hosting).
- An installation's data can be exported, upgraded and deleted, with documented retention; see [Operations](https://docs.lumi.nieve.ai/self-hosting/operations).
- Qualification questions, a booking page or in-chat booking, and enquiries delivered by email or webhook are configurable; see [Qualification, booking and enquiries](https://docs.lumi.nieve.ai/configuration/handoff).

## Next: make the outcome useful

- Business onboarding with an answer preview before publishing.
- Service and case-study knowledge useful across different kinds of businesses.
- Handoff to existing customer-management tools.
- Visibility into unanswered questions, enquiry quality, and operating cost.

## Exploring

- Agency management across client installations.
- Additional model providers and deployment options.
- Additional languages and reusable business templates.

These ideas need user evidence before becoming implementation commitments. Improvements to the current experience and documentation are welcome now; see [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).
